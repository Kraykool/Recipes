# brown butter caramel tart
claire saffitz recipe with the proper amounts written, this recipe triggers me since its so good but making it according to the instructions makes it too oily. use good european style butter for this recipe

### recipe

#### ingredients
##### pate brisee
- 2 cups (260g) all-purpose flour
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt (3 g)
- 10 tablespoons unsalted butter
- ¼ cup water, chilled
- 1 large egg yolk 

##### salted brown butter caramel layer
- 1½ cups sugar (+½ cup water)
- alternatively 1 cup sugar ½ cup maple syrup or honey
- 2 tablespoons corn syrup 
- 3 ounces chilled browned unsalted butter, cut into pieces with the browned parts included (info in recipe)
- ⅓ cup heavy cream, warmed
- 1 tablespoon vanilla bean paste (maybe more) 
- 1 teaspoon diamond crystal kosher salt or 3 g of morton kosher salt

##### salted brown butter choco ganache
- 4 ounces semisweet (60% cacao) chocolate, finely chopped
- ½ cup heavy cream
- 1 tablespoon corn syrup 
- 1½ ounces browned unsalted butter, cut into small pieces
- Flaky sea salt

#### process
- brown 2 sticks of butter in a small saucepan until all the water is cooked out and the milk solids start to fry and turn golden brown and it smells nutty. stir constantly as to not burn it
- cool browned butter mixture down all the way, if you arent lazy then stir every few minutes as it cools to room temp to keep the milk solids dispersed throught the butter, if you dont do this you will have to scrape the solids into the caramel and chocolate
##### pate brisee
- now for this part you can use any other tart recipe you want
- combine flour, sugar and salt together thoroughly on the counter and then cut ten tablespoons of butter into cubes and mix them into the flour coating them and breaking down the butter into small pebbles the flour will turn yellow crumbly
- rub the mixture between your hands to process it further into a more uniform consistency then pile it up and make a well in the center
- mix the egg yolk with the ice cold chilled water whisking with a fork then pour the egg mixture into the well and incorporating the flour slowly until the dough comes together into a play doh ball
- take a pastry cutter and scrape the dough ball smearing it against the counter to make it smoother and more uniform then chill in the fridge
- take half the dough and grate it on the large holes of a box grater, spread up the sides of the tart mold in a even layer, then grate the other half and spread it thinly on the bottom ensuring its even
- use a measuring cup to even out the pastry making sure there are no large holes then cut off the excess that is over the top of the mold, put in freezer for five minutes then chill in fridge until oven is preheated to 350
- line tart with foil evenly, MAKE SURE YOU FILL THE CORNERS WELL and fill with pie weights (beans, rice, ceramic pie weights w/e)
- bake at 350 for 15 to 20 mins until the pastry has set a bit, then remove the foil and bake for a further 15 to 20 mins till golden brown
- leave to cool in the pan until its time to add the filling
##### salted brown butter caramel
- add sugar, corn syrup and water to a small saucepan then boil on medium heat while stirring until the sugar has dissolved then stop stirring once the mixture has run clear
- ALTERNATIVELY: do all this but also add the maple syrup or honey, the mixture wont run clear but you will still be able to see the sugar dissolved
- cook until it becomes amber, push as far as you feel comfortable. all the bubbles should be gone and it should be loose. remove saucepan from heat
- add heavy cream in small amounts until its all incorporated then the brown butter about a tablespoon at a time, add vanilla and salt and mix until a thick runny caramel forms.
- immediately add to tart shell before the caramel siezes up
- put in fridge covered to firm up
##### chocolate ganache and finishing touches
- heat heavy cream and corn syrup, whisking until combined, on medium until just under a simmer, when you seem whisps of steam its warm enough
- add chocolate to a heatproof bowl and pour the cream mixture over the chocolate, then add butter in small dollops on top
- let sit for 5 ish minutes until its all melted then whisk until a thick glossy chocolate ganache forms
- pour over caramel and smooth with offset spatula in an even layer
- let sit for a few minutes until its set enough to sprinke with flake salt
- let set in fridge for 30 minutes to an hour
- slice and serve
