# aeropress recipe
this is my preferred aeropress recipe and instructions. kinda hoffmanesque i guess. i like v60 more than this but i make this more often.

## recipe
### ingredients
- 12 g coffee (light roast preferrably)
- 200 g water
- lol

### process
- grind your coffee, i mostly grind small batches at the store, finer end of medium, the finest you would go for filter coffee pretty much for a light roast, coarser for a darker roast
- boil your water, for light roasts you want your water fresh from the boil, if its too intense then the next brew lower the temp 3 degrees C
- put filter in to the press then dump in coffee, tap on counter to even out the layer
- put on a cup on your scale and put the brewer on top
- add the water making sure you wet all the grounds
- take of the scale and add the plunger pushing in about a centimeter
- time 2 minutes
- swirl gently to disrupt the bed
- time 30 seconds
- press gently all the way through the hiss about 30 seconds
