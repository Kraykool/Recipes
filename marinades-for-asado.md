# marinades
these are a few marinades for taco night, good for both chicken and beef. start with these, then make your salsas, then cook your meat, make your tortillas and boom its dinner time. ill run through salsas in a seperate recipe. also im not going to teach you how to cook chicken or beef you should know how to do that.

### recipes
#### ingredients
##### umami bomb
- 20 serrano chiles
- 200g garlic cloves like 4 heads
- 250g soy sauce
- 6 limes juiced
- 175g neutral oil

##### herby marinade
- 1 bunch cilantro
- 8 limes juiced
- 2 packets sazon goya
- 10 cloves of garlic (a whole head)
- ½ of a white onion
- 1 tbsp of vinegar (whatever you like except balsamic)
- as much olive oil as it takes to come together

##### punchy marinade
- ½ onion
- 1 head garlic
- 4 serrano chiles or 5 chile de arbol
- kosher salt

#### process
##### umami bomb
- broil garlic and chiles until blackened and tender on the outside
- peel garlic put in blender with stemmed chiles
- add lime juice, soy sauce, and oil then blend until emulsified.

##### herby marinade
- put everything in the blender and blend

##### punch marinade
- broil the onion and garlic and if you use serranos broil them too
- if you use arbol chiles soak them in hot water from the kettle until they soften
- use the molcajete or mortal and pestle with salt to process into a smooth paste.
- can add oil if wanted
