# fried rice
i like this recipe alot, i generally dont measure for it but i did it this time for you all. literally put whatever you want in your fried rice, this is just what i do most of the time. its damn delicious but not at all traditional. i use day old rice because i have it around, you can use fresh rice just cook it with slightly less water than you would normally use or steam the rice if you are some kind of loser.

### recipe
#### ingredients
- 4 cups cooked long grain rice (basmati or jasmine are my preferred)
- 1 small shallot, diced
- 2 stalks green onion, sliced thin with greens seperated
- as much garlic as you want, minced fine (around 6 cloves)
- as much serrano or jalapeno or habanero depending on spice tolerance, minced fine
- 2 handfuls shiitake mushroom, sliced or diced
- 5 silces beef bacon, cut into strips
- 3-4 eggs
- beef tallow (can use normal oil if you want but this tastes way better)
- light and dark soy sauce or japanese soy sauce
- mirin
- msg

#### process
- preheat wok on high heat and scramble your eggs in a bowl
- add about a tablespoon of tallow or oil then go in with your eggs stirring constantly until ribbons form, take out of wok and set aside
- add bacon and cook until the fat has rendered and the bacon has started to brown then add the mushrooms and cook until they release all their water and start browning, you may need to add more tallow if it looks dry but dont add too much mushrooms will soak up all the fat
- after mushrooms brown add shallot, green onion whites and cook until softened and starting to brown, then add garlic and chiles, stir constantly to avoid burning garlic
- throw in rice and eggs then stir fry on high heat until everything is coated in fat and the ingredients are dispersed properly
- add light soy and mirin along the sides of the wok along with some msg and salt
- add in dark soy until the desired color has been reached
- portion and serve
